Case: Titration of 50 mL of 6M NaOH with 3M HCl
Calculation of mols of NaOH:
n (NaOH) = M x V = 6 mol/L x 0.050 L = 0.3 mol

Calculation of the HCl volume to neutralize 0.3 mol of NaOH:

n(HCl) = M x V, V = n/M = 0.3/3 - 0.1 L = 100 mL
